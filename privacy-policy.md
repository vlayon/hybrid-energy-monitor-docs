# Privacy Policy – Hybrid Energy Monitor

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Android app **Hybrid Energy Monitor** (package `com.hybridflow.monitor`, "the app") uses, and how. The app is developed by **Vladimir Yonchev**, Bulgaria ("we").

**In short:** the app reads data from your car only to show it on your screen. It has **no internet access**, uses **no analytics, no advertising and no user accounts**, and **sends nothing to us by itself**. Data reaches us only if **you** decide to send us a report.

---

## 1. Data the app processes on your device

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| **Vehicle data** from the OBD2 port (speed, engine rpm, battery current, charge, voltages, temperatures, pedal position…), read through your Bluetooth OBD2 adapter | to show the energy flow and the engine and battery pages | processed on your device only; not stored permanently, except the last battery charge level (to show it while the ignition is off) |
| **Your adapter's Bluetooth name and address** | to reconnect to the adapter you chose | stored in the app's settings on your device |
| **App settings** (car model, theme, night brightness, display options) | to remember your choices | stored on your device |
| **Approximate location** (optional, only if you allow it) | to calculate local sunrise and sunset for the automatic day/night mode | used on your device at that moment; **never stored and never sent** |

The app does **not** collect your precise location, contacts, photos, account information, the vehicle identification number (VIN), or any data from other apps.

## 2. Permissions
- **Bluetooth (connect):** to connect to your paired OBD2 adapter. The app does not scan for or connect to other devices.
- **Approximate location (optional):** only for the automatic day/night mode (see above). If you refuse, the app switches at fixed times instead.
- **Storage (only on Android 9 and older):** only to save a report file to your Download folder when you tap "Save to file".

The app has **no internet permission**. It cannot send data over the internet by itself.

## 3. Diagnostic reports (only when you choose to send one)
When you tap **Send report**, the app creates a text file on your device containing:
- the app version, your device's manufacturer and model, and the Android version;
- the selected car model and the app's decoding settings;
- your adapter's name, Bluetooth address, firmware version and connection status;
- the last vehicle values, and the last 500 messages exchanged between the app and the car (technical OBD2 data);
- the results of the "Scan Toyota data codes" function, if you ran it;
- the comment you typed, if any.

The report does **not** contain your location, your VIN or other personal data, unless you type such data into the comment yourself.

The report **leaves your device only if you send it**: with "Share via app" (through an email or messenger app you pick) or by emailing the saved file yourself. If you send it to **support.hybridflow@gmail.com**:
- **purpose:** only to find and fix problems, and to add support for more car models;
- **legal basis:** your consent, given by sending the report;
- **retention:** we keep it while we work on your issue and delete it within 12 months at the latest;
- **sharing:** we do not sell or share reports with anyone. Anonymous technical values, such as data codes of a car model, may be used to improve the app.

The email itself is handled by your email provider and by Google (Gmail), under their own privacy policies.

## 4. Backups
If Android's backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include the app's settings (for example, the chosen adapter and car model) in your personal backup. This is managed by Android and Google, not by us. We cannot access these backups.

## 5. Children
The app is meant for car owners and is not directed at children under 16.

## 6. Your rights
Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask us at any time to **access** or **delete** reports you sent us. Write to **support.hybridflow@gmail.com**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority; in Bulgaria, this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (www.cpdp.bg).

To remove all data the app stores on your device, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android's settings.

## 7. Changes to this policy
If the app's handling of data changes (for example, if paid Pro features are added through Google Play), we will update this policy and its effective date. The current version is always available at https://vlayon.github.io/hybrid-energy-monitor-docs/.
